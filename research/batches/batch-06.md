### Calibration verdict

**`BATCH_F_METHOD_PASS`.** The entry passes the *method* gate: original image directly inspected, independent later work and date located, dated cross-format instance located, and the missing folklore evidence recorded. This verdict does not prove an authored origin or folklore formation. The distinction between name, image, and persona requires source-by-source review: the NDL itself warns that 大頭小僧 and 一つ目小僧 have been conflated with 豆腐小僧. We therefore did not register either as an alias.

## Repeatable admission rule

For future Batch F candidates, keep a dated, located Stage 1 occurrence separate from the claim of invention; require a later, separately credited work for Stage 2 or a dated, specifically located other medium for Stage 3. Reprints and same-edition copies only corroborate the original. Record Stage 0 antecedents and Stage 4 vernacular reports if actually sourced; mark them unresolved otherwise. Admit only claims about what a specific source depicts or states. Keep the stage assessment in the research log and do not use media circulation to assert oral folklore.
