## Correction and formation review

`claim_0026` is the concrete source-URL correction candidate: its recorded location correctly names 2400120, while its source URL opens 2400113. The two cards belong to the same 1922 article but have different excerpt locations and narrative content. The three `sekiei` cache misses have exact-ID alternate official cards; their registered URLs need access remediation, not a content correction. Kiyohime/Okiku-style later name formation, Oiwa's posthumous spirit account, and Osakabehime's fusion show why a later explanation cannot be promoted to an original attestation without reading the cited publication.
