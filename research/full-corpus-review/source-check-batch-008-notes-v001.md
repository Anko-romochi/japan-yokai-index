## Identity and chronology review

Kyoto Atago Tarōbō and Shiga Akagamiyama Tarōbō occur in different mountain, shrine and story contexts. No source in this batch establishes that they are one individual; keep the existing separation. For Namahage, Oga City's source supports ritual practice and a local earliest-record assertion, while the 1811 visit and later publication/transmission must stay separate. Inugami Gyōbu's appearance with Inō Butayū's mallet is supported as a **later tourism retelling** only.

That chronology response was rejected; source text controls the status above.
