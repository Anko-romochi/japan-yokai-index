# Full-corpus claim source check — batch 036

Checked 2026-09-28. Claims 0471–0479: **nine new checks**. Registered Mie prefectural retellings were read directly. These are later interpretive/public-facing texts; none establishes the tale's first attestation or independently verifies a supernatural event, original object, festival origin or chronology. No corpus edits.

| 0477 | [「川立ち地蔵の不思議な夢」](https://www.bunka.pref.mie.lg.jp/minwa/hokusei/nagasima/index.htm), 西川輪中 and 喜助's dream | The Jizō image is moved from wetland to levee, then in a dream asks to be returned because it saves drowning people. 川立ち地蔵 and 尻冷やし地蔵 are alternative *story labels* for the image. | 0478 | [「延命地蔵」](https://www.bunka.pref.mie.lg.jp/minwa/chusei/misato/index.htm), 下ノ郷 shrine and wartime paragraph | The retelling says all local conscripts returned and villagers **then called** the image 延命地蔵. | 0479 | [「八百比丘尼」](https://www.bunka.pref.mie.lg.jp/minwa/chusei/ano/index.htm), お里 and mermaid meat episodes | 草生's お里 consumes mermaid meat, stops aging, becomes a nun and travels. The later title 八百比丘尼 arises within this tale. This is a regional narrative, not proof that every same-name 八百比丘尼 story refers to one historical person. |

**Review candidates:** 0471 is a fox collective. 0475 contains two figures on one image and a separate testing stone. 0476 is one pillar bearing six realms. 0478 presents a postwar name formation account that needs independent confirmation before it becomes historical chronology. 0479 must remain distinct from other same-name regional narratives absent identity evidence. No automatic correction.
