## Review candidates

The first three records connect child-warning rumors, but do not establish a shared person or common origin. `claim_0108` requires special care because a historical woman died in the narrated episode; the pre-existing fear should remain distinct from her identity. `claim_0112` has a possible card-label versus source-wording question. The two 送り狼 cards show materially different local behavior, while ベトベトさん and ゴウリキさん demonstrate comparable speech rituals across different named regions without proving identity. All source-publication years above remain separate from narrated event dates and claimed earlier objects.
