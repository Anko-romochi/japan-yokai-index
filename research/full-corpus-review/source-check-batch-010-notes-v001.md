## Cross-claim cautions

The re-read batch-004 claims `claim_0094` and `claim_0095` remain supported, respectively, by [Sōgo Reido's 縁起](https://sougoreidou.or.jp/sougoreidou/engi/) (「我が国の代表的義民」 through 「現在のお墓」) and [Murata Ryūdō's article](https://da.lib.kobe-u.ac.jp/da/kernel/81001556/81001556.pdf) (*近代* 99, 2007, PDF pp.2–3 / printed pp.1–2). The temple says the honorific 宗吾道閑居士 was conferred in 1752; Murata distinguishes an attested farmer/execution from an uncertain direct-petition link. The latter PDF text layer is OCR-scrambled and was read cautiously. The 1752 honorific is a name-formation date, and neither page directly exposes the early trial record.

The Sōgo temple's petition narrative and Murata's historical-evidence assessment are different layers; neither cancels the other by fiat. Literary foxes and tengu are described through later performance guides or index cards, so early formation remains unresolved. `claim_0105` requires separation of a picture's approximate date from a cataloguer's later prose. The single source access failure is retained as `indexed_excerpt_only` rather than silently treated as direct verification.
