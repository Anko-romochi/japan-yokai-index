## Later reception and retelling

A page for [浄土寺 on the 南知多三十三観音霊場 site](https://www.minami-chita33.jp/joudoji) directly presents the story under 「お亀さんのいわれ」 and calls the enshrined figure 龍亀大菩薩. It locates the event at 小佐の海岸, gives 明治42年9月10日, and recounts the monk's repeated dreams, the turtle with 「奉大海龍大神」 and 谷村佐助's name, and Suke's dream/ritual involving a turtle at 二見ヶ浦. It frames the narrative as the history of the temple's popular 「お亀さん」 devotion and describes present-day petitions for health and longevity. This is direct evidence of a current local religious/institutional retelling, not independent confirmation of a 1909 event. The webpage has no publication date in the page text inspected; its relation to the 1995 article or temple records is unestablished.

A [JAF Navi temple profile](https://drive.jafnavi.jp/spots/231123X00438/) at lines 16–22 and 50–54 gives another present-day tourist retelling. It also uses 「龍亀大菩薩」, repeats the dream and turtle account, and lists the present address as 愛知県知多郡南知多町豊浜小佐郷1. Its publication date and dependence on the temple/pilgrimage text are unknown. A tourism guide is later-reuse evidence, not an independent early source.

- **Entity grain:** the 1995 record moves among an encountered turtle, a dream-visiting old man, the inscription 「大海龍大神」, a named temple enshrinement, and a present devotional figure. This supports a formation-trace candidate for names and roles, but not Level 3: the early account is unread, and the modern pages may depend on one another or on a shared temple source.
- **Chronology:** 1995 is the registered article publication date; 明治42年9月10日 / 1909-09-10 is the alleged story date; the webpages are undated in the inspected page text. These dates remain distinct.
- **Geography:** Nichibun card 2130010's area field says 愛知県・知多郡・美浜町, while the later temple/pilgrimage and JAF pages place the shrine at 南知多町豊浜小佐郷1.
