## Cross-case review

The cluster exposes a recurrent **historical-person / dramatic-spirit / local-veneration** boundary. The Noh pages support work-specific stage personas, while museum, shrine and local-government pages often summarize older narratives from a later standpoint. For Sakura Sōgo, the shrine account and a 2007 scholarly article answer different questions and cannot be counted as two independent proofs of direct petition. The Koka URL remains the one direct-source gap in this batch. No alias, entity type, or claim correction is made without human review; the URL and possible identity-grain refinements remain research candidates.
