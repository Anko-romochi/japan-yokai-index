Checked 2026-09-28. Claims 0400–0409, ten new checks. Registered sources were read directly. Mie pages are prefectural **retellings**; Chiba pages are prefectural **repostings** of 斉藤弥四郎's `ふるさと民話さんぽ` in specific `広報おおたき` issues. None of those original newsletter pages was read. A Chiba web update date of 2024-02-19 does not date the story, object or original issue. No corpus changes.
