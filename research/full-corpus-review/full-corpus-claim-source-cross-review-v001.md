## Chronology, place, and persona safeguards

- A publication, a cited earlier book, a story's purported event date, and modern reuse are four different dates. For example, `claim_1024`'s 1563 黒法師山 place-name notice does not establish the 1582 tengu persona at 1563; the same later page's `昭和37（1904）` is internally inconsistent. `claim_1049` separates an orally reported モエレ沼 dragon from a 1993 festival dragon and a 1943 amulet.
- Preserve exact regional wording in the individual batches. A page's location is not a proof of origin, nationwide distribution, or identity with same-named beings elsewhere. The 6 宗像 list entries in `claim_0336`–`claim_0341` remain index-level until their cited source numbers are inspected separately.
- A tale's self-identification, a teller's inference, and an editor's later label are different evidence. The batches flag transformations, inferred animal explanations, historical-person identifications, image/persona reuse, and naming changes without equating them automatically.
