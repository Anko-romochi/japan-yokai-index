The [Munakata City museum column](https://www.city.munakata.lg.jp/searoad/kiji0034323/index.html) separately describes 長太郎（ちょうたろう） as head of local kappa and a **shape-disguised kappa** in the 垂水峠 story. It says 樽見峠 is the tale's former writing and 垂水峠 is the later writing. These are later institutional explanations, not the readings or original wording of references 30/31. Do not merge the named 長太郎 with the anonymous pass kappa.

For `claim_0336`, a separate [regional heritage archive entry, reference 宗像市『宗像遺産 暮らし遺産編』(2009)](https://www.munakata-archives.asia/frmSearchBunkazaiDetail.aspx?id=330) recounts six murdered women and **six appearing ghosts**, followed by six Jizō. Its title is 「菊姫の怨霊」, but the narrated apparitions are plural. This strengthens the existing *entity-grain review candidate*: the list title alone should not establish one exclusive named ghost. It is later explanatory reuse, not a directly read copy of catalog reference 29 and not proof of 1550s supernatural events. The current narrow `claim_0336` that the city list has that title remains an index observation; the `named_supernatural_entity` type remains for human review.

Next evidence gate: obtain and page-check the three printed works, record exact story headings and pages, and compare whether the museum/archive retellings derive from them. No six-claim source-depth promotion or correction is made here.
