It does not verify the 1922 printed pages, a historical Genpei eyewitness, or the dating of a living tanuki.

The single `source_0014.source_url` instead opens [card 2400113](https://www.nichibun.ac.jp/cgi-bin/YoukaiDB3/youkai_card.cgi?ID=2400113), excerpt pp.281–282, which supports the separate 金長 and 六右衛門 claims and only mentions the 禿狸 as mediator. Both cards index separate excerpts of the **same 1922 article**; they are not independent publications. Replacing the source URL with 2400120 alone would break the first two direct claim links. A later human-reviewed corpus design must preserve all three card locators and the shared article relationship.
